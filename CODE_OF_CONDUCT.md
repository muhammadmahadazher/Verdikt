# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment of any kind, public or private
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## A project-specific note

Verdikt's output is frequently unwelcome — it exists to tell people their conclusion is weaker
than they wrote. Disagreement about statistics is expected and healthy. Argue about the method,
the assumptions, and the numbers. Do not make it about the person who ran the eval.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers via a GitHub issue
or security advisory. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
